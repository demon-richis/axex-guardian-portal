# Security Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-26

Report vulnerabilities privately to [CONTACT EMAIL]. If GitHub private vulnerability reporting is enabled for this repository, you may use that channel instead. Do not post exploitable details in a public issue.

Include the affected service/version or commit, steps to reproduce, security impact, logs or proof of concept with secrets removed, and a safe way to contact you. In scope are the Axex bot, Guardian Portal, account-intelligence service, and AxexOrbit service. Social engineering and denial-of-service testing without permission are out of scope.

Reports are handled on a best-effort basis. We may ask for clarification and will avoid exposing your report before a fix or mitigation is ready.
